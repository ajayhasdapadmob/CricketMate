Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CrickMate. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our platform.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, CrickMate may collect information such as:

  • Name and player information
  • Team and tournament information
  • Contact details provided during registration
  • Match and cricket statistics
  • Information submitted through forms

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate tournaments, manage teams and players, display match scores, maintain statistics, and improve CrickMate services.

4. Tournament Information

Information submitted for tournament participation may be displayed to other users when necessary for team lists, player profiles, fixtures, scorecards, results, rankings and points tables.

5. Payments

If payment information or payment proof is submitted, it may be used to verify tournament registration. CrickMate should not store complete card numbers, CVV numbers or banking passwords.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

CrickMate may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember preferences, sessions, match information and other necessary settings.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration or misuse. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

8. Third-Party Services

CrickMate may use third-party services such as Firebase, hosting providers, analytics services or payment providers. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

CrickMate is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact the CrickMate administration through the official contact page.
